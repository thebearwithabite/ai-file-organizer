INT. RENTED STUDIO KITCHEN — DAWN

A single cardboard box sits half unpacked by the door. Grey winter light
comes through an unwashed window. The fridge hums.

MAYA (34, shoulder-length dark hair parted left, grey knit sweater, a silver
ring on her right hand) stands at the counter. She has been holding the same
envelope long enough that her thumb has worn a soft edge into its corner.

MAYA
(quietly, to no one)
Still here.

She turns it over. Her thumb finds the seal. She does not break it.

She sets it down, precisely, in the middle of the counter — then pushes it a
half-inch away, as if it had moved on its own.

MAYA
(louder, still not moving)
No.

Outside, a bus sighs to a stop. Inside, nothing else does.
